What can we create together?

Creator-led product demonstrations, unboxings, lifestyle stories, and short-form social content. Tell us which channels you’re creating for and we’ll scope the formats around your brief.

What do you need to get started?

A little about your brand, a product link or description, your target audience, and an idea of your budget. If you already have references or a creative brief, include those too.

How much does a project cost?

Each project is quoted individually. Creator requirements, the number of deliverables, editing needs, and usage rights all help define the scope. We’ll agree on a quote before production starts.

How long does production take?

We’ll confirm a timeline with your proposal based on creator availability, product shipping, the scope of production, and approval milestones.

Can we use the content in paid ads?

Tell us where and how you plan to use it. Paid advertising permissions, platforms, duration, territories, and any creator-account advertising access need to be agreed in writing before production.

Are revisions included?

Your proposal will specify the included revision rounds, feedback milestones, and any additional costs for changes outside the approved scope.
